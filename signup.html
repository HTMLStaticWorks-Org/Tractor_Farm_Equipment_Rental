Standard Equipment Rental Terms & Conditions

1. Dry-Rate Fuel Policy

All machinery is dispatched on a dry-fuel basis. Fuel tanks are full at handover and must be topped up with clean HSD diesel upon return or settled at actual depot fuel rates.

2. Operator & Transport

Operators and farm-gate delivery are not included in base machine rates unless explicitly selected and billed in your rental agreement.

3. Security Deposit & Identification

Clients must provide valid Aadhaar, Kisan Credit Card / Land Passbook, and the applicable refundable security deposit prior to machine release.
