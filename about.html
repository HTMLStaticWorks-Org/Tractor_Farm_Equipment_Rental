Engineering Quality Control

Our 50-Point Equipment Maintenance Standards

Every tractor, harvester, and implement passes through our four-stage mechanical bay checklist before handover to the next farmer.

BAY CHECK 01

Engine & PTO Dynamometer Check

Compression check, radiator coolant pressure test, dual air-filter cleaning, and PTO shaft spline torque inspection at 540/540E RPM.

BAY CHECK 02

Hydraulics & Auxiliary Valve Audit

3-point ADDC lift pressure test, quick-coupler O-ring replacement, and transmission/hydraulic oil viscosity verification for reversible ploughs and trailers.

BAY CHECK 03

Blade, Share & Bearing Replacement

Rotavator boron L-blades, MB plough bar points, and harrow sealed bearings are replaced proactively whenever wear exceeds 25%.

BAY CHECK 04

Seed & Laser Precision Calibration

Laser transmitters are bench-calibrated for ±2mm grade accuracy, and seed drill/planter metering rollers are tested for zero seed crushing.
