Operational Policies & Conditions

Mandatory Rental Terms & Inclusions

To maintain transparent pricing for every farmer, fuel, operator wages, transport, and insurance responsibilities are clearly defined below.

1. Fuel Policy (Not Included)

All listed daily, weekly, and monthly rates are Dry Rates (Fuel Excluded). Tractors, harvesters, and diesel pumps are dispatched with a 100% full, sealed fuel tank. Clients must either return the unit with a full tank of clean High-Speed Diesel (HSD) or pay for actual consumed litres at current depot pump rates.

2. Operator Inclusion (Optional)

Base rates cover bare machinery for qualified self-drive operators. If you require an IronField-certified tractor operator, configure it during booking at ₹950 per 8-hour shift (or ₹1,400/day for specialized Claas combine harvester operators).

3. Delivery & Transportation

Yard pickup and return at our NH-44 Karnal hub is free of charge (and free within a 10 km radius for weekly contracts). Beyond 10 km, flatbed trailer or tractor-towed delivery is billed at ₹45 per km (one-way loaded distance).

4. Refundable Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit (₹3,500–₹10,000 for implements; ₹8,000–₹20,000 for tractors; ₹35,000 for combine harvesters) plus original Aadhaar + Kisan Credit Card / Land Passbook verification is required prior to dispatch. Refunded within 2 hours of clean return inspection.

5. Engine Hour Meter & Wear

Daily tractor and harvester rentals include 8 digital engine hours per 24-hour period. Routine blade/shovel edge wear in normal agricultural soil is covered by IronField; damage from rocks, tree stumps, or unauthorized road haulage is chargeable.

6. Insurance & Field Liability

All self-propelled tractors and harvesters carry valid commercial third-party motor insurance. However, crop insurance, operator personal accident cover (when self-operated), and negligent mechanical abuse are not included unless a separate damage-waiver rider is signed.
